ABOUT INNOVEX

Pioneering ideas for
minds that create, build,
and inspire.

InnoveX is more than a student club — it is a space for people who want to explore, create and grow together. We bring students together through hands-on learning, problem-solving challenges, workshops, hackathons, project showcases and innovation activities. Whether you are discovering your strengths, building your first project or leading a team, InnoveX gives you a place to experiment, collaborate and contribute.
